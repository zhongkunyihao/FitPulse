Help & Support

Find an answer fast, or reach our team
How do I cancel my membership?›
Go to Profile → Manage Membership → Renew, then tap "Cancel Subscription". Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period.
How do I book a personal trainer?›
Open Personal Training from the home screen, pick a coach, and tap "Book Session" to choose a time that suits you.
How do I sync my smartwatch?›
Profile → Connected Devices → Add a Device, then follow the prompts. Steps, heart rate and calories sync automatically afterward.
What is your refund policy on the store?›
Unopened supplements can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Just contact support with your order number.
How do I join a live class?›
Tap the Live tab, then "Join Live Class". You can chat and react in real time — or host your own with "Go Live".
